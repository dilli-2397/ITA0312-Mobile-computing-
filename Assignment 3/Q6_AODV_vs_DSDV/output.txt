Q6 - AODV VS DSDV ROUTING COMPARISON
Mobility | Nodes | DSDV periodic updates | AODV route requests | DSDV setup delay | AODV setup delay
Low | 10 | 10 | 2 | 5 ms | 15 ms
Medium | 25 | 25 | 5 | 5 ms | 25 ms
High | 50 | 50 | 10 | 5 ms | 40 ms
DSDV keeps routes ready but sends periodic updates.
AODV discovers routes on demand, reducing idle updates but adding discovery delay.
For frequent topology changes, AODV is generally preferred in this model.
