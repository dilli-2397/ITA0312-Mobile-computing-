4A TASK 5 - POWER STATE POLICY
Active | Screen on/user interaction | Foreground UI; short bounded work
Idle | Screen off/no interaction | Batch jobs and limited sampling
Doze/Suspended | Stationary, unplugged, screen off | System wake events; defer app network work
Charging | Power connected | Batch sync, backup and maintenance
Urgent push: system push service receives event; do not hold permanent wakelock.
At 15% battery: increase stationary sampling interval, batch uploads, defer non-urgent work.
Emergency exception: visible, time-limited foreground operation with bounded wake time.
