Q7 - EMERGENCY NETWORK EXPERIMENT
Nodes | Mobility | Packet delivery ratio | Mean delay (ms) | Routing control messages
10 | Low | 98% | 20 | 12
25 | Medium | 94% | 35 | 38
50 | High | 88% | 60 | 95
Design: wireless ad hoc nodes + AODV + gateway + optional Mobile IP for home-network continuity.
Failure handling: repair routes after link failure; buffer and forward messages after disconnection.
Evaluation: higher mobility increases delay and routing overhead and can reduce packet delivery.
